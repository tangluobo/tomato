Bt 1.10 embedded components

Source: https://github.com/atomashpolskiy/bt
Tag: bt-parent-1.10

The build obtains and merges the pure-Java bt-core, bt-bencoding, bt-dht and
bt-http-tracker-client artifacts. The two BtModuleProvider service entries are
combined by this project. No native executable or external BitTorrent client
is used.
